## About Google Keep

Google Keep is a free note-taking app that lets you create, edit, organise, and share notes. Use it to keep track of your thoughts, ideas, and tasks, whether that's a to-do list, a set of priorities, meeting notes, or anything else you need to remember.

Keep is available on **Android**, **iOS**, and the **web**. To use it, sign in with your Google account.

## Features

Keep has a number of features that make it a powerful note-taking app:

- **Multiple note formats:** Create notes as text, images, checklists, voice recordings, or drawings.
- **Labels:** Organise notes by topic or project.
- **Colour coding:** Give notes a colour so they're easier to find at a glance.
- **Reminders:** Get notified about a note at a set date and time.
- **Sharing:** Add collaborators so others can view and edit your notes.

## Getting started

1. Download Google Keep from the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.keep) (Android) or the App Store (iOS).
2. Open the app and sign in with your Google account.
3. Tap the **+** button to create your first note.

The toolbar at the bottom of the screen gives you quick access to each note type:

![Google Keep toolbar showing the Lists, Paint, Record Voice, and Insert Image buttons, with the New Note button on the right](images/new-note-toolbar.png)

## Creating notes

| Note type | How to create it |
|-----------|------------------|
| Text | Tap **+** and type your note into the text field. |
| Checklist | Tap the **Lists** icon and start adding items. |
| Voice | Tap the **Record Voice** (microphone) icon and start speaking. Keep saves the recording and a transcript. |
| Image | Tap the **Insert Image** icon, then take a photo or choose one from your device. |
| Drawing | Tap the **Paint** icon and start sketching. |

> **Tip:** You can also create and edit notes in your browser at [keep.google.com](https://keep.google.com/). Notes sync automatically across all your devices.

## Organising notes

<img src="images/icon-label.png" alt="Label icon" width="40">

**To add a label:**

1. Open the note.
2. Tap the **⋮** (More) menu, then tap **Labels**.
3. Select an existing label, or type a new one.

**To colour a note:**

1. Open the note.
2. Tap the **palette** icon.
3. Select a colour.

## Sharing notes

<img src="images/icon-share.png" alt="Share icon" width="40">

**To add a collaborator:**

1. Open the note.
2. Tap the **⋮** (More) menu, then tap **Collaborator**.
3. Enter the name or email address of the person you want to share with, then tap **Save**.

Collaborators can view and edit the note, and changes appear for everyone.

**To send a copy of a note** to another app, such as email or a messaging app, tap **⋮** (More) and then tap **Send**.

## Setting reminders

<img src="images/icon-reminder.png" alt="Reminder icon" width="40">

1. Open the note.
2. Tap the **reminder** (bell) icon.
3. Choose a date and time.
4. To repeat the reminder, select a repeat option such as daily or weekly.

## Searching for notes

<img src="images/icon-search.png" alt="Search icon" width="40">

Tap the search bar at the top of the screen and type your search terms. You can find notes by their text, or filter them by label, colour, or type (such as lists or images).

## Archiving notes

<img src="images/icon-archive.png" alt="Archive icon" width="40">

Archiving moves a note off your main screen without deleting it, so you can keep things tidy and still refer back later.

- **To archive a note:** Open it and tap the **Archive** icon. On mobile, you can also swipe the note to the side.
- **To view archived notes:** Open the menu (☰) and tap **Archive**.
